The longer version

I'm from Pennsylvania, and I came down to LSU for computer engineering. I finished the B.S. in May 2026 and stayed for an M.S. in electrical and computer engineering, focused on communications and signal processing.

Since 2020 I've also done title research as a land agent, which mostly means reading old courthouse records until they agree with each other. It turns out that's decent training for signal processing. The answer is usually in the part nobody wanted to read.

The work that hooked me was RAMpage, a combat robot where I wrote the code that fired the plow, and a radio link I built on two software-defined radios for my communications design course. That radio project is why Dr. Aldo Duarte recommended me for the Office of Naval Research project I work on now.

Websites happened on the side. I built one for our family's nonprofit, then for a few small businesses, and now I keep four of them running.

Why underwater

It started with my little brother.

Adam drowned in our backyard pool at 14 months old, with a house full of family. He survived. In 2020 my siblings and I started Because of ADAM to share everything we wish we'd known.

A pool alarm listens for a splash. A child in trouble barely makes one. I want to know if a hydrophone can hear the difference, so that's what I'm working on, on my own time.
