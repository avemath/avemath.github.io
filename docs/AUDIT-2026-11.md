## Top five for Ave

1. **Drawings shrink below 13px on phones.** SVG labels scale with their figure: the RAMpage timing rows reach 6.6px at 390, the research drawings 7px, the LED wiring 8px, the case study hero art labels 6 to 7px. Recommend: under 560px, wide figures keep a fixed minimum width and scroll sideways inside their frame (the hop plot already does), and the decorative hero-art captions hide.
2. **Two heading styles.** H1s are weight 600 on case studies, About, Services and the résumé, 800 elsewhere, and range from 44 to 84px. I matched Work, About and Contact to the case study size. Recommend one interior H1 (the case study size, one weight), with the home hero, research, 404 and résumé as deliberate exceptions.
3. **Duplicate figure numbers on the home page.** The V2V How it works tab labels its plots with the report's numbers (Fig. 02 to 09, 12), so home has two Fig. 02s, and the V2V case study jumps 09 to 12. Recommend: in the tab, "Stage 1 / PRE correlation, Run 4 (report Fig. 02)"; keep report numbers in the case study, with a line saying so.
4. **"AI" on the PLV Abstract Builder page.** The copy says "An optional AI reading step" and the screenshots show a "Read with AI" toggle. Hard rule 2 bans mentioning AI tools. Recommend deciding whether it covers a client product's feature; if it does, say "an optional automatic reading step" and crop the toggle.
5. **Petro Land Ventures says "Basins mapped 5"** but the text names four (Appalachian, Gulf Coast, Permian, Michigan); the fifth appears only in a screenshot. Recommend naming the fifth in the Context paragraph.
