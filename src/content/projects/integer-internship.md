## Context

Integer Technologies builds software for autonomous surface and underwater vehicles, and it runs an office on LSU's campus so its engineers can work directly with university researchers.

## My role

I was an engineering intern on the Baton Rouge team. My main project was the acoustic characterization of a thruster on a test stand in the lab tank: I planned the measurements, ran them, and analyzed the recordings. I also worked with the team's underwater network simulation tools.

This is also where my work on the Office of Naval Research project [Intelligent Data Management for Distributed Naval Platforms](/work/onr-intelligent-data-management/) began. I continue on a different part of it at LSU.

## What I can share

The details of this work belong to Integer and its customers, so this page stays at the level above.
