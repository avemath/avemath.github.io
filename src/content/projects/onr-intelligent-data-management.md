## Context

The Navy and Marine Corps are moving toward distributed maritime operations: many smaller platforms instead of a few large ships. Those platforms include autonomous vehicles on the surface and underwater, and each one collects far more data than it can send over the links it has.

In 2024 the Office of Naval Research funded Integer Technologies and LSU to work on that problem. The public contract announcement describes it as "the development of decision-making tools for data management on board distributed naval platforms." LSU's announcement puts the core question in Dr. Wei's words: "What and how to exchange critical information between these vehicles are the central questions we will address in this project."

## My role

I started on this project in summer 2026, during my internship at Integer Technologies. I'm continuing on a different part of the same project, Intelligent Data Management for Distributed Naval Platforms, as a graduate research assistant at LSU, advised by Dr. Shuangqing Wei. Dr. Aldo Duarte recommended me for the research post based on my software-defined radio work in his communications design course. My thesis-track M.S. is focused on communications and signal processing, which is where I contribute.
